# Voice & AI guide

How the avatar sounds and thinks:

- **API Keys (BYOK)** — connect your own TTS provider credentials; stored encrypted server-side, never sent to browsers or embeds.
- **Voice** — per-avatar voice picker and synthesis tuning for the connected provider.
- **Chat Providers** — choose which configured AI provider generates conversational replies.
- **UI & Styles** — configure the Begin call-to-action button and spoken captions visitors see.

Live guide: https://adam-speaks.com/guides/voice-and-ai
