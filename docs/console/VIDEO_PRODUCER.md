# Video Producer guide

Record avatar videos in the browser — for social, landing pages, and places a live embed does not fit:

- **Script** — write spoken lines with expression and animation cues, or generate a full AI draft from the video's title, persona, and tone (10 credits per draft, auto-refunded on failure).
- **Video info & canvas** — metadata, recording dimensions, and optional background audio.
- **Publish & record** — opens a producer window; capture the tab while the avatar performs with real lip-sync, expressions, and animation, then download the video.

Live guide: https://adam-speaks.com/guides/producer
